# Hard Sell — Talking Points

Read this before the stall opens. These are the angles to hit when someone asks "why should I switch?"

---

## 1. You're Running Bloatware

- Windows ships with preinstalled apps you never asked for (Candy Crush, TikTok, Xbox Game Bar)
- Telemetry runs in the background sending data to Microsoft — and you can't fully disable it
- Start menu has ads. Your lock screen has ads. You paid for this OS.
- macOS is cleaner but locks you into Apple's ecosystem and hardware

**Say this:** "You know those apps you never installed that show up in your start menu? That's not a bug. That's the business model."

---

## 2. Your Laptop Is Dying Faster Than It Should

- Windows eats 4-6GB RAM at idle doing nothing
- Linux eats 400MB-1GB RAM at idle
- That 4-year-old laptop running Windows? It'll feel brand new on Linux
- Less resource usage = less heat = longer battery = longer hardware lifespan

**Say this:** "Windows is using half your RAM just to exist. Linux lets your hardware actually breathe."

---

## 3. You Already Use Linux

- Android is Linux
- Most servers run Linux
- Smart TVs, routers, Docker containers — all Linux
- The only device you don't control is your laptop. That's by design.

**Say this:** "You're already living on Linux. Your laptop is the one thing you haven't freed yet."

---

## 4. Every Pixel Is Yours

- Rebind any key to anything — no PowerToys, no third-party apps
- Theme everything — browser, terminal, login screen, boot screen
- Choose your own workflow — tiling, floating, keyboard-only, mouse-only, whatever you want
- No one decides what features you get. You decide.

**Say this:** "On Windows, you use what Microsoft gives you. On Linux, you use what you build."

---

## 5. Privacy Is Not a Setting — It's the Default

- No forced Microsoft account
- No cloud lock-in
- No telemetry you have to dig through 15 menus to (maybe) disable
- Your files are yours. Your data is yours.

**Say this:** "Linux doesn't need to know who you are to work. That's the whole point."

---

## 6. It's a Career Move

- Most servers, cloud infrastructure, and DevOps runs on Linux
- Knowing the terminal is a genuine skill employers look for
- Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD — all terminal-first, all Linux-native
- Even if you're not in tech, terminal fluency makes you faster at everything

**Say this:** "Learning Linux isn't a hobby. It's the single most useful skill you can add to your resume this year."

---

## Quick Deflections

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "But I need Office/Adobe" | "LibreOffice handles 90% of it. And there are web alternatives that work better than the desktop apps." |
| "Linux is too hard" | "You're literally watching me use it right now. How hard does this look?" |
| "Gaming doesn't work on Linux" | "Steam Deck runs Linux. Proton runs most Windows games. Check ProtonDB." |
| "I don't have time to learn" | "You spent 3 hours customizing your phone wallpaper. This is the same thing, but for your whole computer." |
| "My hardware won't work" | "Bring it to our workshop. We'll check. Most stuff just works." |
